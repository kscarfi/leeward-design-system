# RecipientRow

A saved recipient: avatar, name, bank and masked IBAN. Choosing the row starts a transfer to them.

## When to use

- Recipient pickers in a transfer flow.
- Lists of saved recipients and favorites.

## When not to use

- Past transactions: use TransactionRow.
- Your own accounts: use AccountCard.

## Anatomy

- Avatar with initials, or a photo or logo.
- Name, with a star for favorites.
- Bank name and masked IBAN.
- Chevron when the row opens a step or a page.

## Content guidelines

- Use the name the person saved, not the legal account holder name, when they differ.
- Always show the last four IBAN digits so people can tell two recipients apart.

## Behavior and responsive

- With `onClick` the row is a button; with `href` it is a link; without either it is static.
- Long names and bank names truncate; the hit area covers the whole row.

## Do

- Put favorites first.
- Use inside List so rows get dividers and spacing.

## Don't

- Do not show the full IBAN in a list.
- Do not make the row and a button inside it do different things.

## Accessibility

- The IBAN is masked visually and read as "IBAN ending in 1 3 2 6".
- The favorite star has text for screen readers, not color or shape alone.
- The avatar is decorative because the name is next to it.

# Code of conduct

Leeward is built in the open, and everyone who takes part is expected to make it a respectful place.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Make room for people who are new, and for people who use assistive technology.
- Accept a decision you disagree with once it has been discussed, and say so if you think it should be revisited.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer, Kemal Salih Carfi, through the contact details on [kscarfi.com](https://kscarfi.com). Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block people who break this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

The icons in `packages/react/src/components/Icon/icons.generated.ts` come from the **Solar Icons Set** by [480 Design](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1166831539721848736), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They were taken from the maintained package by Hakim Saoudi, [github.com/saoudi-h/solar-icons](https://github.com/saoudi-h/solar-icons) (MIT).

Changes: a curated subset of 142 icons in three styles, colors replaced with `currentColor`, path coordinates rounded to two decimals.

## Color ramps

Most primitive ramps in `packages/tokens/source/ramps.json` are based on the [Tailwind CSS](https://github.com/tailwindlabs/tailwindcss) color palette (MIT). The custom ramps (lapisBlue, purpleHaze, oceanBlue, coralRed, richBlack, pumpkin and the extended 950 to 1000 steps) were designed by Kemal Salih Carfi.
